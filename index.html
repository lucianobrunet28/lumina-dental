Ask a question
How do I book an appointment?

Appointments are booked by WhatsApp only, at (555) 555-0123. We'll reply and find the day and time that works best for you.

Do you accept insurance?

We're a private-pay clinic. Message us and we'll send you the details for the treatment you need.

I have an emergency. Can you see me?

Message us on WhatsApp and tell us what's going on. We'll do our best to fit you in as soon as possible during opening hours.

Do you see children?

Yes. We offer pediatric dentistry, with calm visits and lots of patience so little ones feel comfortable.

I'm afraid of the dentist. What should I do?

Let us know in your first message. We explain every step before we do it and go at your pace. Many of our patients arrived nervous and now visit with total peace of mind.

Where are you located?

At 1234 Central Ave, Suite 2, Demo City. We're open Monday to Friday 9 am – 6 pm and Saturday 9 am – 4 pm.
